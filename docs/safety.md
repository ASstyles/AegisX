# AEGISX Safety Architecture & Synthetic Data Boundary

AEGISX is a safe, controlled adversarial research and simulation laboratory.

---

## 1. Strict Synthetic Isolation

1. **Zero Real Customer PII**: All names (e.g. Priya Sharma, Rahul Verma), accounts, phone numbers, and addresses are 100% synthetically generated.
2. **Zero Real Financial Credentials**: No actual Credit/Debit PANs, CVVs, PINs, or banking tokens are used or generated.
3. **No External Payment Rails**: The simulation does not connect to Visa, Mastercard, NPCI/UPI, SWIFT, or any real banking clearinghouse.
4. **No Deployable Attack Infrastructure**: Social engineering, voice cloning, and deepfake KYC vectors are represented strictly as safe behavioral metadata and transaction simulations.

---

## 2. Research & Educational Boundary

AEGISX is designed exclusively for defensive research, adversarial model evaluation, and cybersecurity training.
